# NanoURL - Simple URL Shortener

A modern and user-friendly URL shortening service. Convert your long URLs into short, shareable links instantly.

## 🌟 Live Demo

Visit [NanoURL](https://www.nanourl-dev.xyz) to try it out!

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 Instant URL shortening
- 📋 One-click copy to clipboard
- 🎯 Direct redirection to original URLs
- 💫 Clean, modern interface
- ⚡ Fast and reliable
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design

## 📝 How to Use

1. **Shorten a URL**
  - Visit [NanoURL](https://www.nanourl-dev.xyz)
  - Paste your long URL into the input field
  - Click "Shorten URL"
  - Get your shortened link instantly!

2. **Copy Your Short URL**
  - Click the copy icon next to your shortened URL
  - Share it anywhere you want!

3. **Use Your Shortened URL**
  - Share your shortened link with others
  - When someone clicks the link, they'll be automatically redirected to your original URL

## ❓ FAQ

### Is it free to use?
Yes, NanoURL is completely free to use!

### How long do the links last?
Links are permanent and will not expire.

### Are there any usage limits?
Currently, there are no limits on the number of URLs you can shorten.

### Is it secure?
Yes, we use HTTPS for all connections and maintain user privacy.

## 🔒 Privacy

- We do not track or store personal information
